Features in action

Chest Tabs

Add up to eight customizable tabs to supported storage. Search and sort items, expand a tab to 180 slots, and choose which tabs supply crafting materials.

Quick Actions

Assign shortcuts for farm tools, fishing, combat, and mounts.

Player weddings

Become partners, plan a wedding with Bernard, and invite friends to the ceremony.

More demos Crop tooltips, crafting automation, storage moves, and plot planning. Show 4 clips Hide clips

World tooltips

Hover over crops to check growth time and fertilizer details while tending your farm.

Automation Chest

Send finished crafts from supported stations to linked storage automatically.

Storage Movement

Move or pack up supported storage with its items and tab settings intact.

Plot Planner

Outline rectangular or custom farm plots and check their dimensions and tile counts.
